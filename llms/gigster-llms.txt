# Gigster
 
> Gigster is an enterprise software development services company that connects businesses with top-tier global talent for custom software development, AI solutions, and digital transformation. With a network of 50,000+ vetted developers, designers, and product experts, Gigster offers fully-managed teams, dedicated teams, and on-demand talent augmentation — delivering projects on time and on budget, guaranteed.
 
Gigster has completed 1,000+ engagements for leading enterprises across industries including healthcare, finance, and technology. The company is backed by $32M from top Silicon Valley investors. Key differentiators include AI-driven talent matching, a proprietary delivery platform, 95% on-time/on-budget delivery rate, and 60% lower project risk compared to traditional IT approaches. Average team staffing time is under 10 days.
 
Gigster's three engagement models are:
- **Fully Managed**: Gigster takes full ownership from scoping to delivery
- **Dedicated Teams**: Skilled, dedicated teams that scale with your business goals
- **Talent On Demand**: On-demand experts that blend with your existing team
